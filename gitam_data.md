GITAM (Gandhi Institute of Technology and Management) offers undergraduate, postgraduate, and doctoral programs in Engineering, Management, Science, Pharmacy, Law, Architecture, and Humanities.
Admission for B.Tech is primarily through GAT (GITAM Admission Test) or JEE Main/AP EAPCET/TS EAMCET scores.
Applications are submitted online at the official admissions portal.

--- SOURCE: https://www.gitam.edu/fee-structure ---
B.Tech Tuition Fees: Approximately ₹2,50,000 to ₹3,50,000 per year depending on the branch and campus.
Hostel Fees: Ranges from ₹1,00,000 to ₹1,50,000 per year (includes food and accommodation).
Scholarships: GITAM offers merit-based scholarships up to 100% tuition fee waiver based on GAT ranks and JEE Main percentiles.
